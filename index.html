No lift.
No crowd.
Just the line.

Negative Death makes clothes for the people who ride down, then hike back up to do it again.

It started on trails that don’t show up on any map. Dust in everything, roots in the way, and a pile of jackets by the car that all looked the same.

We wanted something that looked like it had already been through it. So we wash it until it fades unevenly, cut it loose enough to move in, and put lettering on the sleeves that wouldn’t look out of place on an old tattoo sheet.

Every drop is a single piece, made in a small run to the pre-orders we take. When the window closes, it’s gone, and the next one starts from scratch.

No seasons, no lookbook shoots in a studio. Just the work and the trail it’s made for.
